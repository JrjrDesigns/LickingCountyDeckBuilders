Solid Value, Done Right

Pressure-Treated Wood Decks
Built to Ohio Standards

A pressure-treated wood deck is still the most cost-effective way to add outdoor living space to a Licking County home. When customers ask us which option makes more sense for their budget, wood is the clear answer if they're working in the $8,000–$15,000 range and plan to maintain it properly.

"Most wood decks we see in Newark and Granville that are failing before their time share one of two problems — footings that weren't deep enough for Ohio's frost line, or lumber that wasn't rated for ground contact. We build them to last from the ground up."

We use premium-grade pressure-treated Southern Yellow Pine throughout — UC4B rated for all ground contact applications. Every footing is drilled to a minimum of 36 inches, below Ohio's frost line, and poured with concrete tube forms for a solid, permanent base that won't heave or shift with the seasons.

  • Premium PT Southern Yellow Pine — UC4B ground contact rated
  • Concrete piers drilled 36+ inches — below Ohio's frost line
  • Galvanized joist hangers and hardware rated for PT lumber
  • Properly flashed ledger connection at the house
  • Built to current Licking County building codes
  • Full site cleanup on final day

No surprises, no line items you didn't know were coming. Here's what every wood deck project includes from first footing to final board.

Permit Filing

We file your building permit with the appropriate Licking County township or municipality. You don't deal with the paperwork — we handle it.

Proper Footings

Concrete tube form piers drilled to a minimum 36-inch depth — below Ohio's frost line. No deck blocks, no shortcuts that cause heaving in spring.

PT Framing & Decking

Premium pressure-treated Southern Yellow Pine throughout. UC4B rated for ground contact applications. Properly spaced, properly crowned, properly fastened.

Railings & Stairs

Wood balusters and top rail, or aluminum rail if preferred. Stair layout designed to code with proper rise-run ratios and handrail height.

Wood Deck Questions — Answered

What Licking County homeowners ask us about pressure-treated wood decks.

A pressure-treated wood deck in Licking County typically runs $8,000–$18,000 for a standard 12×16 to 16×20 deck. Final cost depends on the size, how high the deck sits off grade, your railing choice, and stair configuration. We provide free written estimates — call (740) 527-0222 to schedule yours.
A properly built and regularly maintained PT wood deck lasts 15 to 30 years in Ohio. The big variables are maintenance (staining or sealing every 2–3 years) and build quality (proper footing depth and framing grade). Decks with shallow footings or untreated framing fail much earlier. We build them to last.
Yes. New PT lumber needs to dry out for a few months before it accepts stain — usually until the following spring. After that, staining every 2 to 3 years significantly extends the deck's life and keeps it looking good. Ohio's freeze-thaw cycles and summer humidity accelerate weathering on unprotected wood.
