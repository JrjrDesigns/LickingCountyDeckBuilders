Deck Building in Hebron, Ohio

Hebron and the surrounding Licking Township area offer a range of lot sizes and property types. The June 29, 2012 derecho caused deck damage throughout Licking County, and Hebron was no exception — decks built with proper footings and hardware held up; those built to a lower standard did not. — from older village-style properties on smaller lots to larger newer builds on the outskirts. We work throughout the area and adjust our approach to the specific property.

Projects in Hebron often involve properties where access can be a consideration — particularly on older lots where the gap between houses is tight. We assess site access at every estimate and plan the build accordingly so there are no surprises on day one. Hebron falls under Licking County Building Department jurisdiction for permits. We handle all permit applications — you don't need to navigate it yourself.

Hebron properties near Route 40 sometimes have grade challenges — sloped rear yards that lend themselves to elevated decks. Those builds require more attention to footing depth and beam sizing, which we account for in every estimate.
