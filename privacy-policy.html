Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Licking County Deck Builders is operated by Trailmark Operations LLC. This privacy policy explains what information we collect when you use this website, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide voluntarily when you submit an estimate request form on this site. This may include your first and last name, phone number, and city or township. We do not collect payment information, social security numbers, or any government identification.

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics platforms, or any third-party advertising tools on this website.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through our estimate request forms is used solely to contact you about your deck project. We use it to respond to your inquiry, schedule an on-site estimate, and communicate with you about your project. We do not use your information for marketing to unrelated third parties.

How We Store Your Information

Form submissions are routed through an automated workflow and delivered to a private Gmail inbox accessible only to Trailmark Operations LLC. We do not maintain a separate customer database. Information is retained only as long as necessary to complete your project inquiry.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. If a licensed contractor is engaged to perform work on your project, relevant contact details may be shared with that contractor for the sole purpose of completing your project. We do not share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

Your Rights

You may request that we delete any personal information we hold about you at any time. To make a request, contact us at the email address below. We will respond within a reasonable time and confirm deletion.
