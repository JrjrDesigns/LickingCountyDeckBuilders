Features & Upgrades

Multi-Level Decks

Two or more levels connected by stairs — useful for sloped lots, walkout basements, and properties where you want distinct outdoor zones for dining, lounging, and grilling.

Cable Railing

Stainless cable railing systems that open up the view and give a clean, modern look. Requires proper post sizing and tensioning — we do it right so cables stay taut.

Built-In Seating

Integrated benches and planters that make the space more usable without adding furniture that has to be stored. Can be built to wrap corners or define zones on larger decks.

Step Lighting

Low-voltage LED lighting recessed into risers and post caps — extends the usable hours of your deck and adds significant visual impact at night. We rough in wiring during the build.

Outdoor Kitchens

Built-in grills, countertops, and storage framed in composite or stainless. Requires coordination on rough-in for gas and electrical — we work with your trades or ours.

Pergolas & Shade

Attached and freestanding pergola structures that define outdoor rooms and add real shade. Built to match your house, not bolted on as an afterthought.
