Deck Building in Granville, Ohio

Granville is a distinct market. The village has a strong sense of architectural character, and homeowners here are thoughtful about how additions — including decks — fit the existing structure. We do a lot of work on older homes where the ledger situation requires careful attention and where material choice matters for how the finished product looks against the house.

Cedar and pressure-treated wood remain popular in Granville for older homes where composite can feel out of place. We'll give you an honest read at estimate on what material suits your property — sometimes composite is right, sometimes wood is the better fit for the character of the house. Permits in the village go through the Village of Granville Building Department; surrounding township properties go through Licking County Building Department.

Granville homeowners often have specific ideas about how they want the deck to look in relation to the house. That's a conversation we welcome. We'd rather spend 20 minutes at estimate getting it right than have you unhappy with the result.
