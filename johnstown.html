Deck Building in Johnstown, Ohio

Johnstown sits in the northern tier of Licking County, where properties tend to run larger and the pace of development is slower. We serve the village and the surrounding Harrison Township area — properties that range from older in-town lots to newer rural residential builds.

Projects in Johnstown often have more room to work with than our Newark-area jobs. Larger lots allow for bigger footprints, standalone pergola structures, and outdoor living spaces that wouldn't fit on a smaller suburban lot. We build to the property and the homeowner's goals, not a template.

In Johnstown we tend to see a higher percentage of first-time deck installations — properties that have been owned for a long time but never had a deck built. Those conversations involve more time up front talking about options, materials, and what the space will actually be used for. We don't rush that part.
