What We Find, What We Fix

Deck Repair Done Right —
Not Just Patched Over

Most deck repairs we see in Licking County fall into one of two categories: surface problems that can be genuinely fixed, and surface problems that are symptoms of a deeper structural issue that needs to be addressed properly. The mistake is treating the second category like the first.

"We see a lot of decks in Newark and Pataskala where someone replaced the top boards a few years back but left the joists underneath — which is what actually caused the boards to rot in the first place. The new boards start rotting in the same spots within 3 or 4 years. We inspect underneath before we quote anything."

Every deck repair estimate we provide includes a thorough inspection of the ledger connection, joist condition, footing integrity, and railing structure — not just the boards on top. We tell you honestly what the deck needs and give you written options.

  • Rotted board replacement — individual boards or full deck surface
  • Joist sistering and joist replacement
  • Ledger repair and re-flashing
  • Railing post replacement and railing tightening
  • Stair repair and stringer replacement
  • Composite deck board replacement and panel matching
Common Repairs

Deck Repairs We Handle Throughout Licking County

From minor board replacement to structural joist work — here are the most common deck repairs we handle in Newark, Pataskala, and across Licking County.

Board Replacement

Individual rotted or splintered boards swapped out. We match PT lumber grade and size. If the joist below is compromised, we address that too — not just the surface.

Joist Repair & Sistering

Soft, rotted, or cracked joists repaired by sistering new lumber alongside the damaged member. This is the correct long-term fix — not just replacing boards on top of failing joists.

Ledger Repair & Flashing

The ledger connects your deck to the house. A compromised ledger is a safety issue. We repair or replace the ledger and install proper flashing to prevent future water intrusion.

Railing Repair

Wobbly railing posts, broken balusters, deteriorating top rails. We repair or replace railing components while keeping the rest of the deck intact when possible.

Stair & Stringer Repair

Rotted stair stringers, loose treads, and failing handrails — repaired to code. Stair safety is a real liability and we take it seriously.

Composite Board Replacement

Cracked, faded, or damaged composite boards replaced — we work to match your existing brand and color as closely as possible. Structural framing inspected at the same time.

Deck Repair Questions — Answered

What Licking County homeowners ask us about deck repair vs. replacement.

If the deck boards are rotted but the framing and ledger are solid, board replacement is a genuine option. If the joists are soft, the ledger is compromised, or the footings have shifted — that's a replacement. In our experience, about 60% of "I need deck repair" calls in Licking County turn into replacements after we inspect the structure. We tell you honestly which it is before quoting anything.
Deck repair costs vary widely. Surface board replacement runs $500–$3,000 depending on how many boards and whether any joists need addressing. Railing repair runs $800–$2,500. Structural repair involving ledger replacement or joist sistering is typically $2,000–$6,000. We provide free written estimates for all deck repairs in Licking County.
