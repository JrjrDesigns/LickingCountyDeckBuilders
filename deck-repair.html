Diagnose First.
Fix What's Actually Wrong.

Most deck repair calls start the same way: a homeowner sees a few bad boards and wants them swapped out. That's sometimes the right answer. But in our experience, visible surface damage is often a symptom of a deeper problem — soft joists, a compromised ledger, or footings that have shifted.

We inspect every deck honestly before we quote anything. We pull boards, probe joists, check the ledger connection, and look at the footings. You get a clear picture of what's actually wrong and a straight answer on whether repair or replacement makes more sense for your situation.

We see a lot of boards that have been patched over instead of replaced. A rotted board on top of a rotted joist just means you'll be calling someone again in two years. We do it right the first time.
  • Full structural inspection before any quote
  • Board replacement, joist sistering, and ledger repair
  • Railing replacement and post repair
  • Footing assessment and correction
  • All repair work permitted when required

Repair vs. Replace —
We Tell You Which

About 60% of “I need deck repair” calls we receive in Licking County turn into full replacements after we inspect. The decks that look repairable on the surface often have structural problems that make repair a short-term fix at best.

The other 40% really are repairable — solid frames and ledgers with surface-level damage that's been caught before it spread. We'll tell you honestly which you're dealing with. If repair is the right answer, we'll fix it. If replacement is the right answer, we won't waste your money patching something that needs to come out.

If the framing is solid and the ledger is good, repair makes sense. If the joists are soft, the footings have shifted, or the ledger is rotted or improperly flashed — you're putting good money into a structure that still has fundamental problems.
  • Board replacement using matching or upgraded material
  • Joist sistering to restore structural integrity
  • Ledger repair and proper waterproofing/flashing
  • Post base replacement and footing inspection
  • Railing rebuild to current code requirements
